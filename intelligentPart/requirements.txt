# What does this do? It is an open-source machine learning library developed by Facebook's AI Research lab (FAIR). It provides a flexible and efficient platform for building and training deep learning models. PyTorch offers dynamic computation graphs, which allow for more intuitive and easier debugging compared to static computation graphs used in other frameworks. It supports a wide range of applications, including computer vision, natural language processing, and reinforcement learning. PyTorch also has a strong community and extensive documentation, making it a popular choice among researchers and developers in the field of artificial intelligence.

It also provides pre-trained models that can be fine-tuned for specific tasks, making it a valuable resource for researchers and developers working on computer vision projects.
